Entry for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: line one warns the past self about one specific moment in a single sentence, and line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. The entry is 1–280 characters, per the competition gate.
